Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Comedy Mastermind, operated by Scott Dikkers, Inc., collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application and website at comedymastermind.com. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your email address and encrypted password when you create an account. Payments are processed by Stripe — we do not store your credit card details. We store your conversation history to provide the history feature. We collect basic usage data including session dates and message counts. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain the Service, process payments, display conversation history, enforce usage limits, send transactional emails, respond to support requests, and improve the Service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We implement reasonable security measures, but no method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. AI-Generated Content

Comedy Mastermind uses Claude, an AI by Anthropic, PBC. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy governs their use of this data (anthropic.com/privacy). AI responses are for creative and educational purposes only and are provided without warranty.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. If you cancel, data is retained for 90 days before deletion. Lifetime subscribers' data is retained indefinitely. You may request deletion at any time by contacting support@howtowritefunny.com.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, export your conversation history, and opt out of non-transactional communications. Contact support@howtowritefunny.com to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Comedy Mastermind is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Services

We use Stripe (payments), Supabase (database/auth), Anthropic (AI), and Render (hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.
